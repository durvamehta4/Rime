# Evidence-to-architecture decisions

Status: research recommendations, not measured model improvements. The [103-paper ledger](ledger.csv) and topic reviews contain methods, author-reported results, counter-evidence and limitations. Numbers are not independently reproduced. [Architecture v4](../../ARCHITECTURE_V4.md) defines the proposed implementation and acceptance gates.

## Differences from v3

V3 already separated identity/delivery, fixed proposal budgets, source rendering and real replay. V4 makes the implementation leaner and its evidence contract stricter: shared features first, explicit source/polarity/provenance, calibrated actual FDP as an optional nonmonotonic-policy experiment, native content/restoration separation, a concrete backend coverage hierarchy and a final-generation policy consistent with the PS's no-selection rule. It caps the identity experiment at 80 rather than 100 GPU-hours, assigning the saved 20 to verified synthesis/C1. The total stays 1,000.

The 103 new reviews include corroboration and negative/transfer evidence, not 103 separately adopted modules. In particular, we reject a new foundation-model/tokenizer pretraining run, a universal multi-encoder ensemble, pure global-similarity verification, naive energy/pitch style editing, hard-coded emotion from text, automatic requested-label inheritance and unchecked recursive RL.

## Novelty boundary

TacoSpawn is direct prior art for a stopped-gradient speaker mixture prior. Factor-first captions, shared/layer-aggregated features, local prosody, confidence rejection, compositional audio, pseudo-labeling and synthetic replay already exist. The earlier corpus also includes CycleSpeech, UALM, TAC/TAC2 and ATTEST; the basic reciprocal/timed-schema idea is not new. The PS itself suggests targeted generation, captioner rewards and known audio transformations.

A defensible contribution must therefore come from measured real Hindi/Tamil improvement and cost: does independently checked, weakness-targeted generation beat extra real replay, random verified synthesis, unverified targeting and unchanged-generator synthesis? Does richer C0 caption supervision also improve G1 under a matched budget? Until those tests run, v4 is a research hypothesis with implementation gates and a narrower novelty claim, not a demonstrated winning system.
